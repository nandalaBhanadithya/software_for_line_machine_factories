Part A — Audit of a Real Business (20%)

Business Name: Sri Balaji Sheet Metal & Precision Laser Works (Plot No. 48/B, Phase-1, IDA Jeedimetla / Balanagar, Hyderabad)
Owner Interviewed: Mr. K. Satyanarayana (Founder & Managing Partner, 51 yrs) · Format: 28-min on-site factory audit

Operational Summary

  • Products & Scale: Custom sheet metal fabrication, CNC laser cutting, and hydraulic press bending. 8–14 batch orders/day (~240/month).
  • Order Arrival: 70% WhatsApp (rough sketches with dimensions, .DXF/CAD files); 20% phone calls; 10% walk-in local contractors from Balanagar and Kukatpally with physical samples.
  • Current Tracking: Thick hardbound register on the laser table + magnetic whiteboard on shed wall (`Party Name | Thk | Pcs | Urgent?`). Tally ERP used solely for billing.
  • The #1 Failure & Cost: Drawing revision mismatch. Customer in Cherlapally sends a thickness change on WhatsApp (2.0mm to 2.5mm); owner forgets to tell operator; operator cuts 350 brackets on wrong sheet. Cost: ₹26,500 ruined raw metal + 4 hrs machine downtime (~₹55,000/month loss).

3. Phase 1 Scope (Strict Must-Win Boundary for Diwali)

IN SCOPE:

  1. Mobile Order Intake & Duplicate Guard: Master SKU catalog with standardized units (`pcs`, `dozen`, `set`) and 48-hour duplicate warnings.
  2. Floor Pipeline Visualizer ("Kahan Atka Hai"): 4 stages (`Order Placed`, `In Polishing`, `Packed & Ready`, `Dispatched`) with 1-tap state toggle for Ramesh.
  3. Credit Hold Enforcement: Automatic visual lock preventing dispatch for overdue accounts unless approved by Suresh.
  4. Split-Fulfillment Dispatch Logger: Accurately tracks partial loadings in tempos.
  5. Karigar Metal Weight Reconciler: Input kg vs output pcs/kg calculation for Munshi ji to recover metal leakage.
  6. Suresh's 60-Second Morning Cockpit: Mobile summary screen with the 3 essential reports.

OUT OF SCOPE: Amazon/Flipkart REST APIs, AI sales prediction algorithms, two-way automated Tally sync, machine IoT.

4. Three Reports Suresh Opens Every Morning

  • Report 1: "Kahan Atka Hai" (WIP Bottleneck Tracker): Shows active orders at outside polishers (Pappu, Ramu) >48 hours and shed backlog.

Part D — Content Strategy (12%)

1. LinkedIn Post (138 Words)

Last week I stood on the shop floor of a metal fabrication unit doing ₹8 Cr annually.

The owner’s son proudly showed me their cloud spreadsheets and digital plans.

But on the floor, the senior supervisor was still squinting at a grease-stained pocket diary to figure out which tempo to load.

Why? Because 90% of manufacturing ERPs are built for accountants behind desks, not the person sweating beside the stamping press.

When software requires 12 mandatory dropdowns just to record a partial dispatch, floor supervisors don’t adopt it. They ignore it.

And the owner ends up making 30 phone calls a day asking: "Maal kahan atka hai?"

Real factory tech doesn't replace the supervisor's diary with a 24-column form. It replaces it with two big buttons on WhatsApp or mobile.

Build for the floor, or don't build at all.

2. AI Transparency Note (7 Lines)

• Tools Used: Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Gemini 1.5 Pro via Antigravity assistant for data extraction from WhatsApp/CSV, ER schema drafting, and code scaffolding.
• Where AI was Generic/Wrong: When prompted for solutions, AI initially suggested building automated Amazon/Flipkart REST APIs, machine-learning ARIMA sales prediction models, and full ERP migration.
• What I Did About It: I rejected those recommendations. The data showed online sales were under 8%, and the 2023 ERP failed due to floor complexity. I intervened to strip out all AI/marketplace bloat and restricted Phase 1 strictly to what matters before Diwali: a 2-button mobile floor screen, credit hold logic, and outside metal loss reconciliation.
