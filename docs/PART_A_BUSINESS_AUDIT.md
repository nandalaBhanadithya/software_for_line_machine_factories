### Key Operational Findings

#### 1. What They Make & Order Volume
- **Products:** Custom SS/MS mounting brackets, control panel boxes, sheet metal casings for pharma machinery units in Cherlapally/Patancheru, and laser-cut perforated grills.
- **Volume:** ~8 to 14 distinct batch orders per day (~220–280 custom job orders per month). Average ticket size: ₹12,000 to ₹75,000 per order.

#### 2. How Orders Arrive
- **Channels:** 
  - 70% via WhatsApp (Clients send rough hand-drawn sketches with dimensions, photos of old broken parts, or AutoCAD .DXF files).
  - 20% Phone call followed by material drops.
  - 10% Walk-in fabricators and contractors from Balanagar and Kukatpally bringing physical metal samples.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Order-to-Delivery Process & Responsibilities
1. **Inquiry & Drawing Check:** Satyanarayana (Owner) receives WhatsApp drawings; forwards to Venu (CAD Draftsman/Nesting Operator) to check sheet layout and nesting efficiency.
2. **Quotation & Advance:** Satyanarayana quotes per kg or cutting-meter via WhatsApp message. 30% advance asked (rarely paid before production starts).
3. **Raw Material Issue:** Floor helper pulls MS/SS sheet from the vertical stack; marks sheet thickness and grade with yellow paint/chalk on the metal.
4. **CNC Laser Cutting & Bending:** Jagadeesh (Machine Master) operates the fiber laser cutting machine and hydraulic CNC press brake.
5. **Deburring & Grinding:** 2 contract helpers remove sharp burrs, grind corners, and wipe off cutting oil.
6. **QC & Counting:** Jagadeesh counts the completed batch, straps parts with plastic bands, and stacks them near the main rolling shutter.
7. **Dispatch & Billing:** Customer sends an auto-trolley or Tata Ace. Satyanarayana checks payment; calls his accountant (Rao) to generate a GST E-way bill / delivery challan on Tally; vehicle loaded and gate pass issued.

#### 4. How They Track Work Today
- **No software on the shop floor.**
- A thick red hardbound register (*"Daily Cutting & Bending Log"*) kept on the dusty operator desk next to the laser machine.
- A magnetic whiteboard mounted on the shed wall with 4 columns: `Party Name | Sheet Thk/Grade | Pcs | Urgent?`. Half the entries are smudged with greasy thumbprints.
- Final billing is done on Tally ERP 9 by Accountant Rao inside the small front cabin.

#### 5. The #1 Thing That Goes Wrong & What It Costs
- **The #1 Nightmare: "Drawing Revision Mismatch & Thickness Confusion"**
- *The Incident:* A client in Cherlapally texts a revised drawing on WhatsApp at 11:30 AM changing sheet thickness from 2.0mm to 2.5mm. Satyanarayana is negotiating another deal on a phone call and forgets to inform Jagadeesh on the shop floor. Jagadeesh cuts 350 brackets using the old 2.0mm stock.
- *The Cost:* **₹26,500 in ruined raw steel sheets + 4 hours wasted on the CNC laser bed** that backs up three other urgent customer jobs. Scrap sells at barely ₹38/kg vs raw sheet bought at ₹74/kg. Happens at least twice a month (**Monthly loss: ₹50,000–₹60,000 + damaged customer credibility**).

#### 6. Two Direct Quotes from the Owner (Original Language)

> **Quote 1 (On floor coordination & WhatsApp overload):**  
> *"Sir, subah se shaam tak WhatsApp pe 50 messages aur drawings aate rehte hain. Kaunsi client ne modification bheja, kaunsi revision final hai, floor par Jagadeesh ko batane mein agar 10 minute bhi chook ho gayi na, 25-30 hazaar ka sheet scrap ban jaata hai. Poora din yahi aag bujhane mein nikal jaata hai."*

> **Quote 2 (On why previous software failed):**  
> *"Pichle saal ek software company aayi thi cloud system bechne. Computer pe operator ko job order aur sheet inventory login karne ko bola. Are sir, Jagadeesh ke haath mein cutting oil aur grease laga hota hai, woh machine chalaayega ya mouse pakdega? Jo mere WhatsApp aur is whiteboard se simple na ho, woh floor pe do din bhi nahi tikta."*
