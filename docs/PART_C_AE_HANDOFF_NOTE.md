### 2. The Real Pain (and the Irrefutable Evidence)

Do not pitch "generic factory digitalization." Pitch the exact bleeding wounds from their own WhatsApp and Google Sheet:

1. **The "Kahan Atka Hai" Blind Spot:**
   - *Evidence:* Suresh admitted on the call: *"Customer phone karta hai toh mujhe Ramesh ko phone karna padta hai."* In WhatsApp, customer *Sharma Bartan* asks: *"Bhai gilas kab tak? Diwali ka maal hai"* while Ramesh and Kunal are debating whether it's even allowed to ship.
2. **Untracked Metal Leakage at Outside Polishers:**
   - *Evidence:* Munshi ji logged: *"Karigar Pappu ko 420 kg diya tha, 1180 pcs aaye... Pappu ka 6 kg short."* A 1.4% metal shrinkage on a single lot equals ₹1,800 lost in one day. At 40 tonnes/month across 4 polishers, this is **₹80,000–₹1,20,000 monthly straight out of Suresh's pocket**.
3. **Credit Black Hole Disguised as "Purani Parties":**
   - *Evidence:* Suresh claimed *"Payment koi problem nahi,"* but Kunal had to flag: *"Sharma Karnal ka gilas wala order hold rakhna... old dues 1.8L."* Without a credit check at packing, finished goods sit on the floor eating working capital during the peak Diwali cash crunch.

### 4. Objections to Expect & Exactly How to Handle Them

#### Objection 1 (Suresh): *"2023 mein ₹1.2 lakh doob gaye ERP mein. Software wale bolte hain, baad mein koi use nahi karta."*
- **Counter-Pitch:** *"Suresh ji, pehle wala ERP desktop software tha jo Ramesh ji ke liye nahi, computer operator ke liye bana tha. Hum computer nahi de rahe. Ramesh ji ke phone pe sirf 2 button honge: 'Polish Aaya' aur 'Gaadi me Chadhaya'. Agar Ramesh ji 3 din mein use na karein, humara pilot payment mat dena."*

#### Objection 2 (Suresh): *"Diwali sar par hai, abhi naya system seekhne ka time nahi hai."*
- **Counter-Pitch:** *"Suresh ji, Diwali season hi toh reason hai! Agar abhi nahi lagaya, toh agle 30 din Sharma ji aur Gupta ji ke 50 calls aayenge 'maal kahan hai', aur factory mein duplicate order ban jayenge. Humara Phase 1 sirf 7 din mein live hota hai. Diwali ke beech mein aapke 5-10 ghante daily bachenge."*

#### Objection 3 (Kunal): *"Hum Google Sheet se kaam chala rahe hain, isme kya problem hai?"*
- **Counter-Pitch:** *"Kunal, aapki sheet mein 1st September ko Gupta Traders ka same Kadai order do baar chadh gaya 'repeat msg' likh ke, aur 3rd September ko party ka naam hi missing hai. Sheet mobile pe floor se update nahi hoti. Jab tak aap sheet update karte ho, Ramesh tempo nikaal chuka hota hai."*
